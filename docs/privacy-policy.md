# Privacy Policy for Jukov Player

**Effective date:** August 10, 2026

Jukov Player is a client for Navidrome and other OpenSubsonic-compatible music servers. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Android or iOS app.

## Information we do not collect

We do not operate user accounts or collect your music library, listening history, searches, playlists, Navidrome credentials, or other content from your music server. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, sell personal information, or share personal information for advertising.

## Your music server and on-device data

The app connects directly to the OpenSubsonic server you configure. Information needed for that connection, along with app settings, playback state, cached content, and downloads, may be stored on your device. We do not receive this information. Its handling by your server is governed by the server operator's own privacy practices.

You can remove locally stored app data by signing out where applicable, deleting downloads, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app. Removing data from your music server must be done through that server or its operator.

## Crash reports

We use **Firebase Crashlytics**, a service provided by Google LLC, to understand crashes, non-fatal errors, and app-not-responding events and to improve app stability. Crashlytics automatically receives diagnostic information when such events occur. Depending on the platform and event, this may include:

- a Crashlytics installation identifier and Firebase installation identifier;
- the time of the event and an app session identifier;
- the app identifier and version;
- device model, operating system version, device state, and hardware information; and
- crash stack traces, exception details, and related technical diagnostics.

We do not intentionally attach your name, email address, Navidrome credentials, music library, or listening activity to Crashlytics reports. We use crash information only to diagnose problems and improve reliability.

Google processes this information on our behalf under its applicable terms and privacy practices. Google states that Crashlytics retains crash stack traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning deletion from live and backup systems. For more information, see [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Because Crashlytics reports use installation identifiers rather than an account with us, we may not be able to identify or delete a particular person's report. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes data stored locally by the app, but does not delete reports already sent to Crashlytics.

## Data security

We take reasonable steps to limit the information handled by the app and to use Crashlytics only for app stability. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and the security of your connection to a music server also depends on that server and the network configuration you choose.

## Children's privacy

Jukov Player is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Crashlytics diagnostic information may be generated whenever the app is used, regardless of the user's age.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's data practices change. The updated version will be published in this repository with a new effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue in the [Jukov Player GitHub repository](https://github.com/jukov/jukov-player/issues).
